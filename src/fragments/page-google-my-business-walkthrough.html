How to add RGS as a Manager to your GMB Profile

Steps:

(If you do not have a Google My Business (GMB) account already set up please create a Google My Business account here.)

  1. On your computer, sign in to Google My Business.
    • If you have multiple locations, open the location you’d like to manage.
  2. In the menu on the left, click Users.
  3. At the top right, click Invite new users .
  4. Enter the RGS email address: rgs-clients@gmail.com
  5. To select the user’s role, click Choose a roleManager.
  6. Click Invite. An invitation will be sent to RGS and we will have access to this account.

(Once we accept this invitation, you will be notified through email.)
